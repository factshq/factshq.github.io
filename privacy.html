Privacy policy – Facts HQ stats

Facts HQ stats is a private tool used only by the owner of the Facts HQ YouTube channel. With the owner's authorization, it reads statistics of the owner's own channel (views, likes, comments, watch time, subscribers gained). Access is read-only: it cannot publish, edit or delete anything.

The data is only used to track the channel's performance in a private spreadsheet. It is not sold, shared or used for advertising. Access can be revoked at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
